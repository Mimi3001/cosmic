Mercury

The first planet in the solar system, low gravity light atmosphere and closest to the Sun.

Venus

Second planet has a thicker atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet.

Earth

The third planet is cool enough for seasonal swings and is rich in silicon.

Mars

The fourth planet has many oceans and various terrain. Its atmosphere is rich in carbon and oxygen.

Jupiter

Planet 5 is bigger than the previous, rich in metals, but colder atmosphere.

Saturn

The biggest planet of the solar system. Has many minerals and is rich in water.

Uranus

Last planet on this solar system, less light but bigger oceans and dense atmosphere.
